Shivang Raikar

Hi, I am Shivang. I live in San Francisco and work as a Founding Software Engineer at OHM, where we keep EV chargers and HVAC systems running across the country. I build the products that make that happen "Ohmie", our multi-agent AI; the mobile app our field technicians carry; and the platform that ties all operations together.

Joining a startup at day one means everything I ship starts as an empty repo and has to reach real users, so far that's apps on the App Store and Play Store, client-facing APIs, and AI systems in production. Before OHM, I built RAG-powered document intelligence at Steam Works Studio, growing it from a K-12 prototype to 200+ daily users across insurance, clinical, and legal.

Outside of work, I write about AI and engineering on Medium and live for hackathons, 10+ as a competitor, most recently on the other side of the table as a judge at AWS Builder Loft.

Fun fact: I am a multi-sport personality, any game going, count me in. Also on the roster: cooking, dancing, the gym, and a little DJing just for fun.
